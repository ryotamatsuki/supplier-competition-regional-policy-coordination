# Stage 8 — Prior-Art Traceability

Auction-side prior art: Li (2005), Szech (2011), Xu and Li (2019), Li and Zheng (2009).

Local procurement / incidence: Vagstad (1995), Marion (2007), Saito and Mitsuta (2012), Macatangay (2016), OECD (2026).

Industrial policy / government competition: Slattery (2025), Motta and Polo (2026), Van Biesebroeck and Zhang (2026).

The lower-stage rent-dissipation result is explicitly imported rather than claimed as novel. The surviving novelty claim is the cross-layer chain from supplier competition to opposite policy thresholds and destruction of differentiated regional-policy Nash equilibria.
