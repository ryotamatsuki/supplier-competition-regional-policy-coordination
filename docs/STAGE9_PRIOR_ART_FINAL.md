# Stage 9 — Final Prior-Art Collision Audit

## Final novelty verdict
`EXACT COLLISION NOT FOUND`

The final audit separates the manuscript’s known ingredients from the surviving contribution.

## Known lower-stage ingredients
- Li (2005) and Xu & Li (2019): expected winning supplier rent falls with bidder count under DRHR; Xu & Li also analyze divergence between procurement-side/private and social competition incentives.
- Szech (2011): private and social bidder-attraction objectives can differ.
- Li & Zheng (2009): endogenous participation can overturn simple bidder-count comparative statics.
- Vagstad (1995), Marion (2007), Athey et al. (2013), Saito & Mitsuta (2012): procurement rules can redistribute surplus and interact with local or favored supplier objectives.
- Macatangay (2016): local-content policy can depend on local-supplier competitiveness.
- Eckersley et al. (2023): actual local procurement has a territorial sourcing dimension.

None of these lower-stage results is claimed as new.

## Closest government / regional-policy work
- Justman, Thisse & van Ypersele (2005) show that regions can differentiate infrastructure to soften fiscal competition for investors. This is direct prior art for strategic regional differentiation, but its mechanism is interregional competition over investors, not supplier competition changing local rent incidence.
- Slattery (2025) studies state/local governments bidding for firms and rent transfer from governments to firms. The strategic direction is government competition -> firm rents; the present paper studies firm/supplier competition -> government-policy equilibrium.
- Recent local-industrial-policy and IJIO industrial-policy work establishes that market structure, localization, and coordination are legitimate policy objects, but does not derive the paper’s equilibrium-set reversal.

## Paper 1 / companion manuscript
At fixed N, the present model maps into the companion paper’s reduced-form binary switching logic via

`G^red = A G_N`, `lambda_N = rho R_N/G_N`.

Therefore the fixed-N incidence wedge is explicitly not claimed as new.

## Surviving novelty
The exact chain not found in the inspected prior art is:

`supplier-market competition N increases`
`-> total complementary surplus G_N rises`
`-> locally internalized supplier rent rho R_N falls`
`-> coordinated differentiation threshold falls`
`-> decentralized differentiation threshold rises`
`-> differentiated Nash equilibria can disappear`
`-> unique decentralized duplication can coexist with coordinated differentiation`.

The paper’s defensible novelty is therefore the **market-structure-induced change in the exact regional-policy equilibrium set**, not any individual auction or decentralization component.

## Final kill test
A paper that already derives all of the following would reopen novelty: supplier-count competition, opposite social/local surplus movements, strategic complementary regional roles, opposite policy thresholds, and destruction of differentiated policy Nash equilibria. Stage 9 did not identify such a paper.
