## Verdict
`PASS — DISCLOSE EXPLICITLY TO EDITOR`

## Companion manuscript
`Industrial Policy Composition and Regional Value Chains`

## Overlap that is openly conceded
At a fixed supplier count N, the present paper’s unilateral switching conditions can be written in the companion paper’s reduced-form binary logic using

`G^red = A G_N`

and

`lambda_N = rho R_N/G_N`.

Thus the fixed-N incomplete-capture wedge is not a distinct contribution of Paper 2.

## Substantive separation
### Companion / Paper 1
- continuous fixed-capacity policy composition;
- complementary surplus and incidence largely primitive;
- no supplier auction;
- no supplier-count comparative static;
- no private-information procurement continuation.

### Present Paper 2
- binary regional role choice;
- explicit private-information procurement continuation;
- total surplus and local incidence derived from order statistics;
- supplier-market structure N is the comparative-static primitive;
- increasing N can change the exact Nash-equilibrium set and eliminate differentiated equilibria.

## Editorial disclosure
The manuscript already cites and explains the companion relation. Stage 9 adds a cover-letter disclosure that repeats the distinction in plain language. This is recommended even if the submission portal does not specifically request a related-manuscript upload.

## Salami-slicing kill test
Could the present paper be reduced to one robustness subsection of Paper 1 without changing Paper 1’s model class?

`NO.`

Doing so requires firms, private costs, an auction mechanism, order statistics, a new market-structure primitive, Bayesian continuation values, and equilibrium-set comparative statics. The two manuscripts share a reduced-form fixed-N intuition but answer different economic questions.
