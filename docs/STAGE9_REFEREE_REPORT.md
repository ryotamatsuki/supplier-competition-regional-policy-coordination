# Stage 9 — Independent Final Referee Report

## Recommendation
`ACCEPT FOR SUBMISSION / SEND TO IJIO`

This is a submission-authorization judgment, not a prediction of editorial acceptance.

## 1. Mathematical integrity
The government game, full boundary mixed-equilibrium correspondence, high-regime mixed equilibrium, coordinated benchmark, competition–coordination divergence, wedge nesting, asymptotic reversal, and reserve/trade-failure proof have been independently reconstructed. No fatal mathematical error was found.

Stage 8’s boundary correction is exact. Stage 9 adds an independent regression script rather than relying on the manuscript’s own verification script.

## 2. Economic mechanism
The mechanism is coherent and deliberately narrow:

- more suppliers improve the production-side match by lowering expected minimum cost;
- under the maintained DRHR condition, more suppliers reduce winning supplier information rent;
- the coordinated benchmark values full real project surplus;
- the D-region government internalizes only its modeled local share of supplier-side surplus;
- these forces move the coordinated and decentralized role thresholds in opposite directions.

The result is not that competition directly reduces welfare. It is that competition changes the incidence supporting decentralized role selection.

## 3. Novelty
The individual ingredients are known. The paper correctly concedes:
- auction rent dissipation;
- private/social bidder-count divergence;
- local procurement/favoritism motives;
- strategic regional differentiation;
- the fixed-N incomplete-capture wedge.

The surviving contribution is the cross-layer equilibrium result: supplier-market competition can eliminate differentiated regional-policy Nash equilibria while differentiation remains coordinated-optimal. No exact prior-art collision was identified in the final search.

## 4. Paper 1 separation
The related companion manuscript is not hidden. The fixed-N mapping is explicitly conceded. The present paper requires a different model layer—private-information procurement and the N comparative static—and is sufficiently distinct for separate submission, provided the relationship is disclosed to the editor. Stage 9 adds a cover-letter disclosure.

## 5. IJIO fit
The paper’s central comparative static is market structure and the continuation is a procurement market. IJIO remains a natural first outlet. The principal editorial risk is contribution magnitude, not topic ownership or correctness.

A desk editor may still view the combination of known ingredients as too small. The correct response to such a rejection would be to follow the frozen fallback ladder rather than add endogenous entry or another model margin merely for prestige.

## 6. Presentation
The first page now clearly states that rent dissipation is standard and that the contribution is an equilibrium-set effect. Stage 9 adds two useful prior-art anchors:
- Justman, Thisse & van Ypersele (2005) on regional differentiation;
- Eckersley et al. (2023) on the territorial dimension of procurement.

These additions sharpen rather than weaken the novelty boundary.
